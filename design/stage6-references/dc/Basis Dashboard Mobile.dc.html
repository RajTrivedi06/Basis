Δ = median deviation vs the all-provider market median

How the deviation is computed: for every (day, SKU) pair with at least three offers, each provider's median quote is compared to the all-provider median; the figure shown is the median of those daily deviations across the provider's history.

A caution: Vast.ai aggregates thousands of independent sellers, so its deviation is a marketplace aggregate, not a pricing policy. All figures are quoted prices, not transactions.

01Premise

Basis is a variance-accounting study, not a price index. Collect quoted prices, normalize them into a canonical schema, decompose the variance into observable factors versus a residual. The residual is the finding.

If a benchmark designer can't tell you which adjustments produced the headline number, the headline number is doing the wrong job.

02Data collection

Twice-daily pulls from four source APIs — Vast.ai REST, RunPod GraphQL, AWS Spot history, TensorDock REST. Raw responses are stored immutably, forever: every derived number traces back to an original payload.

03Canonical schema

Normalization is conservative and rule-based. A GPU string either matches a known rule and maps to one of 96 canonical SKUs, or it is skipped and logged — never guessed. Missing values remain an explicit UNKNOWN group; missing data is a category, not a gap.

04Dispersion metrics

Spread is summarized by median and interquartile range. A (day, SKU) pair enters only with at least three offers — below that, a single listing can masquerade as a market. Thin samples are excluded, not smoothed.

05Basis decomposition

Sequential ANOVA, in fixed model order. Each factor is credited with the variance it explains after the factors before it.

Order dependence. Reordering redistributes credit among factors — but never changes the residual.

Why log prices. A $0.10 gap means something different at $0.30/hr than at $6/hr. Ratios, not absolute differences, are the meaningful metric.

07What the residual means

The residual is the basis risk a compute benchmark would have to absorb. Roughly 60% of variance is unexplained with marketplace listings included — around 82% without. Most of the price of a GPU-hour is not accounted for by where it is, how it's committed, who sells it, or what's bundled with it.
